World Map Quiz

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

World Map Quiz ("the game") is made by Andreas Fick Løjtnant Krusell ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information about you when you play. If anything is unclear, write to grntxt@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We don't run servers that collect anything about you.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your own phone only.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information from your device to show and measure those ads. Where the law requires it, you're asked first, and you can change your answer at any time.
  • There is nothing to buy in the game.

We are responsible ("data controller") for the game. Google is responsible for the information it collects through AdMob, under its own privacy policy.

Information saved on your phone only

The game saves the following on your device. It is never sent to us:

  • Your best scores, your stamps and the dates you collected them.
  • Your settings: language, distance units, sound, vibration and similar choices.
  • How many stamps you have used today.

You can delete your progress under Settings → Reset progress. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads: a short ad after a finished round, and a longer ad you can choose to watch for more stamps. The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google.

To show ads, count them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location (for example your country or city).
  • Information about your device and the game, such as the model, operating system, language and app version.
  • How you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped), and crash and performance information about the ad software.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before your information is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. If you don't agree, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised.

You can also reset your advertising ID, or ask apps not to use it for personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or similar, depending on your phone).

Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Sharing pictures

When you share a stamp or your passport, the picture is made on your phone and sent with the app you choose, such as a messaging app. We don't receive it. The message contains a link to the game in Google Play.

Reading names aloud

Names are read by recordings included in the game, or by your phone's own text-to-speech voice. If your phone's voice is used, the speech service on your phone handles it under its own terms. The game doesn't send anything to us.

Why and on what legal basis

  • Showing ads, counting them and preventing fraud: our legitimate interest in offering the game for free, and your consent where the law requires it.
  • Personalised ads: your consent.

Transfers outside the EU

Google may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

How long information is kept

Information on your phone stays until you reset your progress or uninstall the game. Google keeps the information it collects according to its own retention policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your information, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable form, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting what happened before. Because we don't hold information about you on our own systems, most requests about ad information are best sent to Google. You're always welcome to contact us at grntxt@gmail.com and we'll help.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark that is Datatilsynet (www.datatilsynet.dk). You can also contact the authority in the country where you live.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content for a general audience. In the EU, if you are younger than the age of digital consent in your country (13 in Denmark), a parent or guardian should make the choice in the consent form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gets new features. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important, the game will tell you.
